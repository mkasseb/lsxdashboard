What do these risks mean?
Storm outlook
Marginal, Slight, Enhanced, Moderate and High describe increasing severe-storm potential. The fraction is a category rank, not a probability or a warning.
Flood outlook
Marginal, Slight, Moderate and High describe flash-flood risk from excessive rainfall. River flood levels are shown separately in the gauges card.
Fire outlook
Elevated, Critical and Extremely Critical describe conditions that support rapid fire spread. Avoid outdoor burning when fire risk is elevated.
Severe-weather probabilities
The chance of a tornado, damaging storm wind or large hail within 25 miles during the stated period. A low probability still allows an event.
Outlooks and warnings
Outlooks support planning. An official NWS warning calls for action now; follow its instructions.
